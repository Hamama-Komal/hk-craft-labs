Why this exists

I kept meeting people with good ideas and no one to ask.

For the last two years I've been building Android apps in Flutter and working with AI: language models, chatbots that answer from your own documents, tools that take the boring parts of a job away. I've shipped nine apps to Google Play.

Along the way I noticed that almost everyone has an idea like this: an app for their shop, a quiz for their class, a way to stop drowning in messages. Most of those ideas never go anywhere, because people don't know where to start or who to trust with them.

So I started HK Craft Labs. It's small on purpose. I'm a woman building this from a small city, on my own terms, and I want it to be a place where anyone feels comfortable walking in with a half-formed idea, whatever their background.

— Hamama

How it goes

Three steps, and the first two are free.

  1. 1

    You say hello

    A message, a DM, a voice note on WhatsApp, whatever's easiest. A rough idea is plenty.

  2. 2

    We talk it through

    I ask questions and give honest answers, including "you don't need an app for this" when it's true.

  3. 3

    If you want, we build

    A clear written plan with scope, timeline and price first. Then builds you can try on your own phone as we go.

My promises

What you can count on.

  • You'll hear back from me

    Every message is read and answered by me personally, not a bot or an assistant.

  • Your idea stays yours

    I won't share or reuse what you tell me. Happy to sign an NDA before you go into details.

  • No pressure, ever

    Talking to me doesn't commit you to anything. Plenty of good conversations end with advice and nothing else.

  • You own what we build

    The code, the app listing and the accounts are in your name by the end of the project.
