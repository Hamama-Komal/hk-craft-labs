Privacy policy

Your information, in plain words.

Effective 22 November 2026

HK Craft Labs ("we", "us") is a small studio run by Hamama Komal in Bhakkar, Pakistan. We build Android apps that are published on Google Play. This policy explains what information our apps and this website handle, why, and what control you have over it. The short version: we collect as little as we can, we never sell your data, and you can always ask us to delete it.

Who this covers

This policy applies to every app published under the HK Craft Labs developer name on Google Play, and to this website. Each app's Google Play listing also has a Data safety section that summarises what that particular app collects. If an app ever needs to handle information in a way that isn't described here, we will say so inside the app before it happens.

What we collect

Things you create in our apps

Photos, audio recordings, notes, edits and other files you make or choose in our apps are processed on your device. We don't upload them to our servers. If a feature ever needs to send something off your device (for example, to an AI service to check grammar), the app tells you before it does, and the content is used only to give you that result.

Device permissions

Some apps ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos and media, or location. We only ask when a feature you're using needs it. For example, a voice recorder needs the microphone and a land-measuring app needs your location. You can turn any permission off at any time in your phone's Settings → Apps.

Usage and crash information

Some of our apps use Google Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, such as which screens are opened, your device model and Android version, and what went wrong when the app crashed. This helps us fix bugs. It does not include your name, your files or your messages.

Advertising

Some free apps show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and general information about your device to show and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).

Purchases

If an app offers in-app purchases or subscriptions, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or bank details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made.

When you contact us

If you email us, message us on WhatsApp, Instagram or TikTok, or use the form on this website, we receive whatever you choose to send: usually your name, your contact details and your message.

How we use it

  • To make the app's features work, like recording, editing, saving or measuring.
  • To find and fix crashes and improve the apps.
  • To show ads in free apps, where an app includes them.
  • To reply to you when you get in touch, and to give you support.

We do not use your information for anything else, and we never sell it.

Who we share it with

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information. The only outside companies involved are the services that help our apps run, and only in the apps that use them:

  • Google Play for distribution, purchases and subscriptions.
  • Google Firebase for analytics and crash reporting. Firebase privacy information
  • Google AdMob for ads. How Google uses information from ads

These companies handle data under their own privacy policies, including Google's Privacy Policy. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Files you create stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Anonymous analytics and crash data are kept by Firebase for a limited period under Google's retention settings. Messages you send us are kept only as long as we need them to help you, and are deleted sooner if you ask.

Children

Our apps are meant for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Turn app permissions on or off in your phone's settings.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads.
  • Uninstall an app to remove its data from your device.
  • Email us to ask what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted. We'll reply within 30 days.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, trackers or analytics, and it doesn't store anything you type into the contact form. That form only opens your own email or WhatsApp app with your message filled in. Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser so the site remembers it. The site is hosted on Vercel and uses Google Fonts, which may record standard technical information such as your IP address when the page loads.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also mention it in the affected apps.
